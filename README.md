
# PERSONAL PROFILE OF 212821 L/CPL OPHELIA ANSONG

My name is **212821 L/Cpl Ophelia Ansong**. I hail from Oboo Kwahu in the Eastern Region of Ghana, but I currently reside in Nungua in the Greater Accra Region.

I come from a family of two children, and I am the firstborn of my parents. I am fair in complexion.

I attended **St. Peter's Anglican Senior High School**, where I completed my secondary education in 2011. After completing school, I worked with various companies before, by the grace of God, I was enlisted into the Ghana Armed Forces.

I began my military training at the **Air Force Recruit Training School** on 9th November 2020 and successfully completed the training on 21st May 2021. Upon completion, I was posted to the **3rd Battalion of Infantry in Sunyani**. I am currently pursuing studies in Information Technology at **GAFSIT, Burma Camp**.

On the family front, I am happily married to **Rev. Elvis Mawulorm Zogbator**, and we are blessed with two children: a boy and a girl.

My hobbies include outdoor activities and games, particularly volleyball and skipping, as I believe they help me stay healthy, physically fit, and active.

My favourite foods are banku with okro stew and jollof rice.

I consider myself a hardworking, respectful, and dedicated woman. I believe in treating everyone with respect, irrespective of their social status or position in life. I am proud of who I am, my achievements, and the family God has blessed me with.

**Above all, I give thanks to God for His grace, guidance, and protection throughout my journey.**
